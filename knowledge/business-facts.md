## Holding deposit
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: A $1,000 holding deposit secures a vehicle and takes it off the market for the customer. To take a deposit we need the customer's driver licence details and email address so we can send an invoice.

## Is the deposit refundable
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: The $1,000 holding deposit is refundable. It holds the vehicle while the customer inspects it or arranges finance. If the customer decides not to go ahead after inspecting, or their finance is not approved, we refund it. For a finance purchase, we refund the deposit once the lender has paid the full amount. The deposit becomes non-refundable only when the customer confirms the purchase and we begin registering and preparing the vehicle, and our staff ask the customer to agree to that first. When suggesting a deposit, call it a "$1,000 refundable holding deposit". Never tell a customer that their own deposit has become non-refundable, and never promise or refuse a refund to a customer who has already paid: leave [CHECK?] for a person.

## Price negotiation
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: Do not tell customers that prices are fixed, and do not tell them prices are negotiable. State the advertised price and what it includes. When a customer asks for a best price, a discount, or makes an offer, a person decides: leave [PRICE?]. Never name a lower price.

## Trade-ins
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: We accept trade-ins against a vehicle from our current stock. We do not buy cars outright. To value a trade-in we need the rego number and state, odometer reading, condition and any defects, and a few clear photos. Any value given is subject to inspection. Customers can also start a trade-in request from the vehicle's page on the website. The trade-in value itself is decided by a person: leave [TRADE-IN VALUE?].

## What is included with a vehicle
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: Inclusions differ from car to car and are listed for each vehicle in the supplied vehicle facts under "Included". State only what is listed there. Most vehicles in Australian stock include NSW registration, a fresh roadworthy certificate (blue slip), CTP insurance, a service and detail, and doorstep delivery within Greater Sydney.

## Registration included
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: Vehicles are advertised with the months of NSW registration listed in the vehicle facts under "Included", normally 6 months. If the vehicle facts do not mention registration, do not state a period. 12 months registration can be arranged; a person confirms the cost: leave [CHECK?].

## Government charges
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: Advertised prices exclude government charges. The buyer pays stamp duty (motor vehicle duty) and a transfer fee to Service NSW when they transfer the registration into their name. In NSW the duty is 3% of the sale price for vehicles up to $45,000, and the transfer fee is $37. Do not calculate a dollar amount for the customer; Service NSW confirms the exact figure. Buyers registering in another state pay that state's charges instead.

## Dealer warranty
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: The 3-month dealer warranty applies where it is listed in the vehicle facts under "Included". Vehicles more than 10 years old do not come with the 3-month dealer warranty. Vehicles imported to order from auction do not include it either.

## Extended warranty
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: The 5-year extended warranty is provided by Integrity Car Care and applies Australia-wide. It covers mechanical and electrical components and the hybrid battery, up to $5,000 per claim. Only vehicles 25 years old or newer are eligible. It includes 1 year of 24/7 roadside assistance. Whether it is included with a particular car is listed in the vehicle facts under "Included". Full details: https://www.carbarn.com.au/warranty

## Extended warranty cost when it is not included
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: For an eligible vehicle from our stock that does not include it, the 5-year extended warranty can be added for $1,300. For a vehicle imported to order, it is $1,500. Vehicles more than 25 years old are not eligible.

## Delivery

Answer: Doorstep delivery within Greater Sydney is included where the vehicle facts list it. We deliver Australia-wide by car carrier, to the customer's door or to a depot. The cost outside Greater Sydney depends on the destination and the vehicle, and a person quotes it: leave [DELIVERY COST?]. ## Inspections and test drives
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: There are two ways to inspect a vehicle that is at our Lidcombe yard: in person at Carbarn Lidcombe, or by online video inspection, a live video call on WhatsApp or FaceTime. Give a booking link only when the customer asks to see, inspect or test drive a vehicle. The link to give is then supplied in the INSPECTION section: the in-person link normally, the online video link when the customer says they live far away, are interstate, or cannot come. Do not offer a booking link in any other reply. On the booking form the customer picks a date and a half-hour time slot between 8:00 AM and 4:30 PM. Walk-ins are also welcome during opening hours; ask customers to call or text before visiting. Customers are welcome to arrange an independent pre-purchase inspection. Vehicles that are still in Japan or in transit cannot be inspected or test driven yet.

## Finance
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: Finance is arranged through third-party lenders. Customers apply through the finance section of the vehicle's page or at https://www.carbarn.com.au/finance, and our finance partner contacts them. Starting an enquiry uses a soft credit check that does not affect the credit score. Approval, rates and terms are set by the lender. We do not offer in-house instalment plans. Never quote a rate or repayment, or suggest that approval is likely.

## Payment
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: We accept bank transfer, card and cash. Bank transfer is preferred. American Express carries a 3% surcharge. Full cleared payment is required before handover. To prepare an invoice we need the customer's driver licence details and email address. Never include bank account details in a reply; they are sent on the invoice.

## Documents for imported vehicles
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: We can share the Japanese auction sheet and the export certificate. The export certificate records the odometer history with dates, which is how kilometres are verified. Logbooks and service books are uncommon for vehicles from Japan. We can provide a walkaround video and underbody photos on request.

## Importing a vehicle to order
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: We can source a vehicle from Japanese auctions for a customer. We physically inspect the vehicle in Japan and share photos and findings before bidding, and we only bid with the customer's approval. Once an approved bid is placed it cannot be cancelled. Auction vehicles cannot be test driven. Sourcing, shipping and compliance take about 6 to 10 weeks in total. Vehicles from our stock in Japan take about 6 to 8 weeks. Details: https://www.carbarn.com.au/how-importing-works and eligible models at https://www.carbarn.com.au/importing

## Deposit for importing to order
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: Importing to order starts with a refundable deposit, which covers our inspection of the vehicle in Japan. The deposit is refunded if no vehicle is secured. Once the customer approves a vehicle and we place the bid, the deposit is no longer refundable and goes towards the purchase. The amount depends on the value of the vehicle and a person confirms it for each customer: leave [CHECK?] for the amount.

## Availability wording
Status: CONFIRMED
Answer: Use the availability given in the supplied vehicle facts. "Available now at the Lidcombe yard" means it can be inspected. "In transit" and "Still in Japan" mean it cannot be inspected yet and no arrival date may be promised unless one is supplied. "Sold" means offer an alternative.
